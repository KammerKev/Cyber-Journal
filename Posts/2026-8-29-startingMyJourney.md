# Twenty Years In, Starting Over on Purpose: My Pivot into DFIR

Over the last 15 years, I have led incident response teams across Counter Terrorism, as well as offensive and defensive cyber operations, working across a range of intelligence disciplines along the way. I bring extensive analytical experience and years of grounding in physical forensics. What I lack, at least at the level I want, is current hands on experience with the tools and processes used in cyber DFIR work.

This post is an honest account of that gap, and the plan I have put together to close it.

## The Honest Assessment

I currently serve in a managerial role focused on the daily operations of data security, including preventing and safeguarding against data loss, whether intentional or inadvertent. I miss the hand on work, and have identified two gaps.

First, my technical skills have gone rusty. Linux, digital forensics tools, and a number of newer processes have slipped simply because I have not needed to run them myself in offensive or defensive contexts for some time. Leading a team requires a different skill set than executing the work directly, and I"m ready to build that back up.

Second, my current position requires validating external vendors, which has me working through the SANS OSINT course toward the GIAC Open Source Intelligence (GOSI) certification. This requirement is not optional, but it also presents a genuine opportunity to sharpen skills that have gone stale.

## The Plan

Over the next three months, I will alternate study nights between two tracks:

- The OSINT course, working toward GOSI
- The SOC Analyst job role path on Hack The Box, covering the fundamentals of incident handling, security monitoring, and other core requirements

Alternating between the two is intended to keep the material fresh and prevent either track from becoming stale through overexposure.

Weekends will be reserved for home lab work. Hands on work has always been where I do my best learning, and rebuilding my Linux fluency, or standing up a home micro server, is work I genuinely look forward to rather than a task to check off.

## The Goal

Two certifications within three months:

- GIAC Open Source Intelligence (GOSI)
- HTB Certified Defensive Security Analyst (CDSA)

Neither certification is the end goal in itself. Both are checkpoints on the path back to hands on technical work.
